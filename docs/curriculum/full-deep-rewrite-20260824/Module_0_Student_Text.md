# PlayIQ Module 0: The Assessment and AI Workspace Orientation

## Your first mission: meet Orion

Welcome to PlayIQ.

Before you learn how to use AI, you are going to learn how to use your own
questions, imagination, judgment, and curiosity.

This first module is not a boring setup form and it is not a test. It is a short
conversation and a set of quick challenges that help Orion understand how you
currently learn, what feels difficult, and what you want to make possible.

By the end of this module, you will have:

- met Orion, your guide for the course;
- created your first Learning Blueprint;
- captured a starting snapshot of how you think and learn;
- identified one subject to rescue and one subject to advance;
- prepared a safe AI workspace;
- learned why better questions create better possibilities;
- unlocked Module 1.

## The Genie Principle

You may have imagined having a genie. If you had one wish, what would you ask
for?

Here is the surprising part: with AI, the question is part of the wish.

AI can help turn a thought into an explanation, a plan, a design, a practice
session, or a first version of an idea. But the usefulness of the result depends
on the question you ask, the context you give, and the way you check the answer.

There is no shortcut to knowledge. There are better ways to learn it.

In PlayIQ, you will learn to:

1. imagine what you want to understand or create;
2. ask a clearer question;
3. use Orion as a teacher and coach;
4. check whether the answer makes sense;
5. turn what you learned into something you can use.

## Lesson 0.4 - Your AI workspace orientation

Now Orion moves from genie to teacher. This is a safe sandbox tour. You are
learning how to communicate with an AI before asking it to do anything
important.

### 1. Name conversations so your context stays clear

Use a separate conversation for a separate subject or project.

Examples:

- `Math - Fractions`
- `Biology - Cell Membrane`
- `History - Causes and Effects`
- `Game Idea - First Design`

Why this matters: a clear conversation gives Orion better context and makes it
easier for you to return to the right work.

### 2. Protect private information

Do not paste passwords, home addresses, private financial details, private
messages, or another person's personal information into an AI chat. Ask a
parent before uploading anything that is not clearly yours to share.

### 3. Ask for a better explanation

If the first answer is not useful, do not give up and do not immediately start
over. Try:

- "Make that simpler."
- "Show me another example."
- "Explain the missing connection."
- "Ask me one question to see what I already understand."
- "Give me a hint, not the answer."

These are small prompts, but they are powerful because they tell the genie what
kind of help you actually need.

### 4. Try text and voice

Choose a short paragraph from a public or school-approved source. Ask Orion to
explain it in text, then try voice mode if your parent has approved it.

Text felt: [ ] clear  [ ] too dense  [ ] useful  [ ] other: ________________

Voice felt: [ ] clear  [ ] distracting  [ ] useful  [ ] other: _______________

Which mode would you choose for a difficult topic, and why?

______________________________________________________________________________

### 5. Attach material safely

If you attach a textbook paragraph or screenshot, check that it does not reveal
private information. Use a small section and ask Orion to explain, question, or
quiz you on it. Do not upload an entire private account, private worksheet, or
someone else's work without permission.

### 6. Find settings and custom instructions

With your parent, locate the platform's settings or custom-instruction area.
You are only finding it today. Do not change important settings until Orion
teaches you why the change matters.

### Pattern interpretation

Use the evidence as a starting clue, not a permanent label.

- If voice helped more, Orion can try verbal summaries first.
- If text helped more, Orion can try written steps first.
- If both worked, you can choose based on the task.
- If neither worked, the next question may be about topic background, pace,
  example size, or the missing connection.

The goal is not to prove that one format is your only learning style. The goal
is to discover what helps you take the next useful step today.

## Learning Supercharger Blueprint update

Your Learning Supercharger Blueprint is a set of starting instructions that
tells Orion how to try helping you. It is not a permanent label about what kind
of learner you are.

This first setting controls how Orion should **begin an explanation**. For
example, Orion might start with a real example, a simple visual layout, short
steps, the big picture, or a question about what you already know.

Orion will test the setting with you. After an explanation, you decide whether
it helped. If it did not, you change it. Your Blueprint becomes more accurate
through evidence from the course—not through guessing once.
