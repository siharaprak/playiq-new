## Source reconciliation

The baseline is the complete Tutor Project pathway source package dated
2026-10-07, Git commit `01cc75552ad9221db41af1c99853e09b52defd32`.
Every source module is included in this revision and the combined reading copy.

The three founder review documents were proposals for evaluation:

- `1 Sienvi Prioritized Fix List.docx`, version 3, October 9;
- `2 First Session Redesign.docx`, version 3, October 10;
- `3 Final Beta Scorecard.docx`, version 3, October 10.

Useful adopted principles include an early visible result, exact practical
directions, save/reopen and fresh-context checks, fewer duplicate records,
and evidence that distinguishes attempted work from successful performance.
Their academic first-session task, length-based module splits, broad product
deferrals, proposed research/grant controls, and unmeasured score thresholds
are not automatically approved curriculum or release changes. Narration,
captions, parent-view policy, and dashboard changes need site-level work;
this text package does not claim those functions were implemented.

The founder documents refer to an older August course and contain no completed
teen beta outcomes. They do not demonstrate that the October source's
Modules 8 or 9 fail because of length. The comparison probabilities discussed
in the meeting were judgment estimates, not measured completion rates.

## Learning and tool accuracy

- The Tutor starts in Module 0. Module 5 is not redefined as its start.
- Character aesthetics are creative choices, not evidence of learning style.
- Learning preferences remain provisional and tied to actual performance.
- Reusable prompts plus reference images are not LoRA model training.
- Image availability, limits, and labels may vary. The tutorial includes a
  truthful text fallback and does not require a new account or paid trial.
- Two unsupported legacy claims were corrected without removing the lessons:
  the Module 1 brain-atrophy claim and the Module 2 claim of learning faster
  than any private tutor in history. The coaching/practice teaching remains.
- Total course duration and a 1-2 hour completion promise are unverified.
