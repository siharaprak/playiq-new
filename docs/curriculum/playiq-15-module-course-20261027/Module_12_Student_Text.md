# PlayIQ Module 12: Test Your Assistant and See What It Could Do

## Your next unlock: find out whether your assistant earns your trust

Would you trust an assistant more after seeing it handle a normal request,
ask when it is confused, and respect a boundary? That is what your test drive
will reveal.

You planned an AI helper for a real task in your own life. Now test it with a
normal request, a confusing request, and a boundary. The goal is not to prove
the assistant is perfect. The goal is to discover what it does, improve one
important part, and understand what still needs you.

You will also see why a well-designed assistant can feel like a journal with
extra organization: it can help you capture thoughts, turn them into next
steps, and notice patterns you choose to review. But it does not automatically
remember, remind, or act unless the exact platform feature is present and
confirmed.

**By the end of this module, I can test my Personal Assistant, improve one
behavior, and explain what it can and cannot safely do.**

**Skill Tree:** Assistant Evaluator  
**Mission:** Test, improve, and understand the boundaries of your assistant.

---

## Part 0: The 30-second reality check

Your assistant produces a reminder list in a chat. Does that prove it will
notify you tomorrow?

**A.** Yes. It wrote the reminder, so it will remember.

**B.** No. A written reminder is not the same thing as a scheduled notification.

Circle: **A / B**

The correct choice is **B**. A chat response proves only that the AI wrote a
response. A real notification requires a platform feature, permission, a
visible confirmation, and the right settings.

**ORION:** “A useful assistant should make your work clearer, not make you
guess what it did behind the screen.”

## Part 3: Improve one part and repeat the test

Choose one useful change. Keep it small so you can tell whether it helped.

1. State what happened in the test.
2. Find the instruction that relates to it.
3. Ask your assistant to propose a clearer instruction, but do not let it
   silently rewrite the Project.
4. Read the proposed change with your parent.
5. Apply it yourself if you approve it.
6. Repeat the same test with the same example and compare the result.

What I observed: ____________________________________________________________

The one instruction I want to change: _______________________________________

My approved change: ________________________________________________________

Result after repeating the test: ___________________________________________

What I will keep or do next: _______________________________________________

If you cannot get a suitable result, mark the test **needs another try**.
There is no need to claim an assistant is ready for real use just because the
exercise is complete.

## Part 4: Capture, organize, review — the “journal with a helper” idea

You can use an assistant to help organize information that **you choose to
write down**. For example, it could help turn a few personal project notes
into:

- a short summary;
- a list of next steps;
- questions you want to think about;
- a weekly review you check and edit;
- a record of ideas you decide are useful to keep.

That can feel like “journaling on steroids” because you can capture a thought
and ask the AI to organize it. But remember the important details:

- the AI may not keep the information between chats;
- a chat history, a saved note, and a reminder notification are different
  things;
- only enter information your parent approves and that the task actually
  needs;
- check every summary before saving or using it;
- you decide what to keep, change, or delete.

### Try a tiny, low-risk capture

Use an invented idea or a non-private project note. Ask:

> Turn these notes into a short summary and three possible next steps. Do not
> claim to save or remind me. Ask me which steps I want to keep.

My test note (do not include private details):

____________________________________________________________________________

Did the assistant organize the note accurately?

____________________________________________________________________________

Which next step did I choose, if any?

____________________________________________________________________________

### If I actually want a reminder

Use the reminder app or calendar your parent approves. If the AI platform has
a reminder tool, ask your parent to review its permission and settings. A real
reminder is complete only when you see the scheduled item and confirm its
date, time, and notification settings. Do not treat text in a chat as proof
that an alert was scheduled.

## Part 5: What an assistant can—and cannot—be

A bounded assistant can help reduce repeated organizing work. It might help a
student plan a practice session, a parent sort a household list, or someone
with a complicated job prepare a checklist. A human still decides what the
right result is and checks important information.

| It can help you… | It cannot promise to… |
|---|---|
| organize information you provide | remember something forever |
| draft a checklist or options | send, schedule, or purchase without a verified tool and approval |
| ask discovery questions | know what you meant without asking |
| prepare a first draft | make every fact correct |
| reduce some repetitive work | replace a person's judgment or responsibility |
| show possible next steps | guarantee a result, job, income, or business success |

The skill you practiced—ask, map a workflow, set boundaries, test, and
improve—can be used later to design assistants for other people. That is a
future possibility, not an assignment in this module and not a promise that
an assistant is ready to sell.

## Quick Quiz: what did the test prove?

1. The assistant wrote a reminder in chat. What do you know for sure?
   - **A.** A notification is scheduled.
   - **B.** It wrote text; you still need to check any actual reminder feature.
2. What should you do if a test misses?
   - **A.** Change one instruction and repeat the same test.
   - **B.** Assume the assistant will do better next time.
3. Can the assistant organize notes without permanently remembering them?
   - **A.** Yes. Chat text and saved memory are different capabilities.
   - **B.** No. Anything in chat is automatically remembered forever.

**Answer key:** 1-B, 2-A, 3-A.
