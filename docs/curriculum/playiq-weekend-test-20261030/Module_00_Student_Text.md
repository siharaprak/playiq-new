## The Genie Principle

**A look ahead, after your parent setup:** imagine turning an idea into a
character you can see, then learning how to direct an AI that helps you make
sense of a difficult topic. In Module 1 you will create that character with a
guided tutorial. Here, your first unlock is finding your starting point and
setting up the private Tutor Project that will travel through the course.

You have probably heard stories about a magical genie in a bottle. If you were handed three wishes right now, what would you ask for?

Here is the secret of artificial intelligence: **The prompt is part of the wish.**

If you ask a genie a vague, sloppy wish like *"make me rich"*, you might end up with a pile of useless Confederate currency or buried under a mountain of heavy gold coins you cannot move. 

AI works the exact same way. If you give an AI a lazy prompt like *"help me with my homework"*, it guesses what you need. Usually, it spits out a bland, robotic essay or a solved math equation that teaches you nothing and sounds like a machine wrote it.

```text
       ┌─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
       │                   THE GENIE PRINCIPLE                   │
       ├─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
       │  Lazy Wish     ➔ Sloppy Guess    ➔ Zero Real Learning   │
       │  Clear Prompt  ➔ Better Guidance  ➔ More Practice     │
       └─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

There is no shortcut to real knowledge. But there are infinitely better paths to learning it.

In PlayIQ, your teaching sequence is always:
1. **Imagine:** Identify the wish, problem, concept, or curiosity in your head.
2. **Clarify:** Write a sharp prompt that sets the rules of engagement.
3. **Engage:** Use Orion to explain, question, hint, quiz, and diagnose confusion.
4. **Verify:** Test whether the AI's answer is accurate, logical, and in your voice.
5. **Build:** Turn the knowledge into a physical build, an essay, a code project, or an artifact you own.

### The Core Architecture: System Prompt vs. User Prompt

To command an AI, you must understand how frontier models actually work under the hood:

```text
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        HOW AI MEMORY IS SPLIT                          │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  SYSTEM INSTRUCTIONS (The "House Rules" / DNA)                        │
│  - Lives in background settings or project instructions.               │
│  - Applies within this workspace when its instructions/context are available.│
│  - Controls: Tone, Role, Guardrails, and How it Explains.              │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  USER PROMPT (The "Daily Visitor" / Task)                              │
│  - What you type in the text box today.                                │
│  - Examples: "Explain question 4", "Give me a hint on this equation". │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Clear instructions can help an AI respond in a more useful and consistent way,
but they do not stop mistakes or hallucinations. You still need to check
important facts and stay in control of what the AI does.

Today, you are going to give your AI **PlayIQ Tutor DNA** and create the one private Tutor Project you will keep using in every module. This is not the PlayIQ website chatbot and it is not your Personal Assistant. Your Tutor Project is a learning tool for you; your Personal Assistant is a separate build in Module 11.

## Continue to Orion's Assessment - what to do next

**Continue to Orion's Assessment** opens the assessment on PlayIQ. Answer the
questions there; you do not need to guess where to type a secret phrase.
When the results are ready, they appear on that page. Read the results, correct
anything inaccurate using the review controls, and use the single **Continue**
action to move forward. A loading message is not a completed result; if an
error appears, retry rather than assuming your answers were saved.

Review the short learning answers you want included in your future Personal
Learning Knowledge File. Use **Save**, wait for confirmation, and reopen the
entry. Your workbook answers and external AI chats do not save themselves to
PlayIQ. Leave unknown results visibly missing.

For the hands-on Tutor conversation, switch separately to your parent-approved
AI, open the same **PlayIQ Tutor Project**, and paste the prompt below plus
only the answers you choose to share. Review its proposed record; then bring
back the approved summary to this module's learning-answer area on PlayIQ.
No course button gives the website access to your external account.

**Prompt to start the external Tutor record:**

> Orion, this is my PlayIQ Module 00 assessment. Help me record only what I
> chose to share. Separate what I reported from what the activity observed.
> Treat learning preferences as hypotheses, not fixed labels. Ask one
> clarification question at a time if needed. Show me a concise proposed
> Course Learning Record entry and exactly what you would add or change. Do
> not claim anything was saved. Wait for my approval.

## Mastery Check — The Boss Handshake Challenge

To officially unlock **Module 1: AI Learning Code**, execute this final challenge inside your new AI workspace:

1. Open your `PlayIQ Tutor Project`.
2. Enter this prompt:
   > *"Orion, I'm ready to start Module 1. Give me a 60-second challenge that tests whether I can turn a lazy question into a coaching question. Don't give me the answer—wait for my reply."*
3. Read the challenge your AI gives you.
4. Type your upgraded question.
5. Check that your revised question asks for coaching rather than a finished answer, and that the AI waits for your attempt. Record what actually happened; AI praise alone is not proof of mastery.

## Save your approved Module 00 learning answers on PlayIQ

Before leaving, return to this module's learning-answer area. Save a short
record of your goals, Rescue and Advance Targets, starting preferences, parent-reviewed boundaries, and what actually happened in the handshake.

1. Enter what you actually did or noticed, separately from what you prefer.
2. Include only a Tutor setting you approve, its source activity, and when to
   retest it. Use **not tested** or **MISSING_INPUT** for an unknown result;
   do not copy example scores as your own.
3. Review the proposed text. Leave private, unrelated, or unapproved details
   out. Ask your parent if unsure, then use **Save** and wait for confirmation.
4. Reopen the saved entry. If it is missing or saving failed, keep your notes
   and retry; do not assume Orion retained them from an external conversation.

These saved, approved answers feed the **Personal Learning Knowledge File**
you review and download in Module 9. This checkpoint uses the work you already
did; it is not another assignment or another Tutor Project. An unrun or
unapproved item stays visibly missing, never guessed or silently filled in.
