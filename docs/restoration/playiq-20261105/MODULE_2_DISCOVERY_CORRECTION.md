# PlayIQ Module 2 Tutor Discovery Correction

Scott previously approved teaching instructions and Knowledge Files in Module 2, followed by an actual copy-and-paste discovery step in the student's external AI. The October 30 Module 2 explains the terms but has no ten-question Tutor interview. Its end-of-module recordkeeping prompt does not replace that practical discovery activity. Apply this correction as part of Scott's complete Module 2 review.

## Place the practical step

After explaining the difference between Project Instructions and a Knowledge File, before later lessons assume the student has collected their learning context, add a short tutorial. It starts the Tutor knowledge journey; it does not build a second Tutor or finish the six-file integration early.

Student-facing instructions:

1. Open another tab and sign in to the AI account your parent approved in Module 00.
2. Open the **same PlayIQ Tutor Project** or the labelled dedicated-conversation fallback. Do not start a new Tutor Project, change account-global instructions, or use the PlayIQ site chat for this interview.
3. Copy the prompt below, paste it into a conversation inside that Tutor Project, and send it.
4. Answer one question at a time. Short answers and “not sure yet” are fine. Use only information you and your parent are comfortable sharing. Where the course already collected an answer, reuse your own approved answer rather than repeating work.
5. Review the proposed learning-context note. Correct mistakes and approve only what you want kept. Preferences are starting hypotheses, not diagnoses.
6. Bring the small approved summary back to Module 2 on PlayIQ and save it using the real learning-answer controls. The private AI chat does not sync to the course. Wait for confirmation and check the entry returns when reopened.
7. Keep the reviewed note with the same Tutor Project/course record. The website's Module 9 download will later collect your approved Modules 00 through 8 answers into the Personal Learning Knowledge File. Do not invent a final file, missing experiment results, or an upload receipt now.

## Copyable discovery prompt

```text
I am taking PlayIQ. We are starting the learning-context notes for the
personal Tutor Project I already created in Module 00. Help me discover what
I need before we build the final Tutor files later in the course.

Keep the Project's existing parent-approved privacy and coaching rules.
Ask these TEN questions ONE AT A TIME and wait for each reply. If I already
provide an approved answer, use it and ask only what is still missing.
Offer a couple of simple examples if I am unsure. Do not ask for passwords,
contact details, private family information, or another person's data.

1. What would you most like this Tutor to help you understand or be able to do?
2. What school level or subject context would be useful, if you want to share it?
3. What subject feels strongest or most interesting, and what would you like
   to explore next in it?
4. What subject or exact step feels harder, and where do you notice getting stuck?
5. When an explanation is confusing, which TWO or THREE approaches would you
   like to try: a spoken explanation if supported, a diagram, an everyday
   example, a story, ordered steps, writing it in your own words, or practice?
   You may prefer different approaches for different tasks.
6. What has actually helped you explain or use an idea so far? Separate a
   course observation from a preference; keep untested approaches untested.
7. What pace and amount of information would you like to test first, and
   what should I do if it starts to feel overwhelming?
8. When you are stuck, how should I ask about your attempt and offer hints
   while keeping the thinking and final work with you?
9. What fictional name, tone, and teaching personality would you like this
   Tutor to use? Keep these design choices separate from learning evidence.
10. What approved notes or learning material should the Tutor be able to use,
    what should stay out, and what would count as a useful first session?

After the interview, show ONE concise proposed learning-context note with:
goals, subject strengths, precise help targets, approaches to test, actual
evidence and source module if supplied, pace/rescue settings to test,
Tutor name/personality, approved reference scope, privacy exclusions, and
missing or not-tested items. Do not invent any of them. Do not label me as
a fixed auditory, visual, or other learner type.

Explain that Project Instructions tell you how to behave; a Knowledge File
supplies reviewed reference context. These notes do not retrain your base model.
Show exactly what you propose to keep or change, then wait for my correction
or approval. Do not claim a file was created, saved, uploaded, or connected to
PlayIQ. We will review, compile, and download the final Tutor materials later.
```
