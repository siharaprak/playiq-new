How to Reset a Student's Account

Operational instructions for Sihara & the Admin Team
Date: Sept 1, 2026
Portal: Admin → Users
Status: Active

1 What Does the "Reset" Button Do?

The new Reset Progress feature gives any student a clean slate from Day 1 (Module 0) with a single click.

What Gets Reset
  • Course Progress: Clears all completed lessons and module nodes.
  • Quizzes & Challenges: Clears previous quiz scores and boss battles.
  • Starting Survey: Lets the student retake their initial Orion assessment.
  • Submitted Work: Clears past practice uploads and proof cards.
What Stays the Same
  • Login & Password: Student uses their existing email and password.
  • Name & Profile: Their account details remain saved.
  • No Re-invites Needed: They can log straight in with no extra setup.
  • Grade Tier: Their assigned difficulty level remains set.

2 How to Reset a Student (3 Simple Steps)

1
Open the Student Roster

Go to playiq-new.vercel.app/admin/users (or click Dashboard → Users in the top menu).

2
Find the Student

Type the student’s name or email into the search bar at the top.

3
Click Reset and Confirm

On the right side of the student's row, click the Cyan Reset Button ↺ Reset.

A popup will ask you to confirm: click Confirm. The student's progress will immediately change to 0%.

3 Quick Reference: Admin Action Buttons

Button / Action What It Does When to Use It
↺ Reset (Cyan) Wipes lessons & quizzes back to Day 1. Keeps login info. Student wants a fresh start or wants to redo the lessons.
Level Dropdown Changes grade track (Elementary, Middle, High, Adult). Adjusting the curriculum difficulty for the student.
⊘ Suspend Temporarily pauses student access to the platform. Putting an account on hold without deleting anything.
🗑 Delete Permanently deletes the entire account. Cannot be undone! Cleaning up a test account or closing an account permanently.
