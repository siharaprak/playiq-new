PlayIQ Module 2 Worksheet: Power, Policies, and
Gray Zones (PROPOSED DRAFT)
Instructions: Complete this worksheet by making active decisions on the scenario cards. PlayIQ is about
building competence, not passive rule-following. Let's navigate the real-world gray zones.
Part 1: The Power Tool Principle (Active Branching Scenario)
1. (Scenario Card) You have a major history report due. You're completely stuck on how to structure it.
You have three choices:
Option A: Prompt the AI: *"Write a 5-paragraph outline for an essay on the causes of the American
Revolution."* Use it to structure your own research and writing.
Option B: Prompt the AI: *"Write the intro and first body paragraph for my history essay."* Copy it and
write the rest yourself.
Option C: Paste a draft you wrote into the AI and prompt: *"Give me feedback on my arguments and
highlight sentences that are confusing."*
Branching Analysis:
Which of these choices is a Superpower that builds your writing skill? Explain why.
Which of these choices is a Superweapon that shortcut-trains your brain to avoid hard work? Explain
why.

Part 4: The Highest Path Test (Real Decisions)
5. (Scenario - The Lab Report) Your lab partner suggests using AI to write the "Discussion" section of
your chemistry report because the class is hard. Apply the Highest Path Test questions:
1. *Does this make me stronger or weaker?*
2. *Would I be proud if a mentor saw this choice?*
3. *Am I using this to create or to escape?*
Your Decision: What is the highest path action you should take, and what do you tell your partner?
Part 5: Social Power & Applied Ethics
6. (Scenario - Pause Before Share) A classmate sends you an AI-edited audio clip of a teacher saying
something funny but completely out of character. They tell you to post it in the class group chat.
Filter Check: Apply the Pause Before Share rules:
1. *Is it true/real?*
2. *Is it respectful?*
3. *Could this hurt someone's job or reputation if shared?*
Your Action: Explain what your response should be to prevent digital harm.
