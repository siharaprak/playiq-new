Module Promise

In this module, you will learn how to get a head start on a school topic before class, before homework, or before a test.

You will not ask AI to do your work.

You will use AI to help you:

- map the topic

- find the core idea

- understand examples

- test yourself

- create useful proof

- save learning fuel for your future Learning Supercharger

A Learning Supercharger is your future personal AI tutor. It will learn how you learn best, but only if you feed it useful
proof.

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Choose a real school topic to pre-learn.

2. Break the topic into a simple map.

3. Find the first principle or core idea.

4. Use examples and non-examples to understand faster.

5. Build five self-test questions.

6. Create a 10-Line Summary.

Lesson 4.2: Identify Gap Type

Big Idea

Not all confusion is the same.

If you know the type of confusion, you can fix it faster.

The 6 Gap Types

1. Word Gap: You do not understand an important word.

2. Background Gap: You are missing an earlier idea.

3. Step Gap: You do not understand the order.

4. Connection Gap: You understand pieces but not how they fit.

5. Overload Gap: Too much information at once.

6. Expectation Gap: You do not understand what the assignment wants.

Prompt to Use

Based on my chunk ratings, ask me 3 short questions to figure out my main gap type. Do not give me the full
explanation yet. Help me identify whether this is a word gap, background gap, step gap, connection gap, overload
gap, or expectation gap.

Activity: Gap Diagnosis Card

Hardest chunk:

My main gap type is:

I know this because:

One clue that showed the gap was:

The best next move is:

Lesson Check

1. What was your main gap type?

2. How did you figure it out?

3. Why is it useful to know the type of confusion?

Lesson 5.2: Safe Simplification

Big Idea

A simple explanation is useful only if it stays accurate.

Bad compression makes the idea sound easy but wrong.

Good compression makes the idea clear and still true.

Examples

Too vague:

Photosynthesis is plants eating sunlight.

Better:

Photosynthesis is how plants use sunlight, water, and carbon dioxide to make sugar for energy.

Too vague:

Fractions are just smaller numbers.

Better:

A fraction shows part of a whole or part of a group.
